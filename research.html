Research

Science, AI and air transportation

A research programme centred on the design, analysis and deployment of AI and data-driven methods for complex, safety-critical transportation systems.

Artificial Intelligence for Air Traffic Management

My research develops AI-based methods for air traffic prediction, optimisation and control under uncertainty, including trajectory prediction, air traffic flow management, airport and airside operations, urban air mobility and mixed-mode airspace integration.

Human–AI Hybrid Systems

I focus on human–AI collaboration rather than automation for its own sake. This includes modelling trust, workload, situational awareness and decision-making; designing explainable and conformal automation; and validating concepts through human-in-the-loop simulation and cognitive experimentation.

Complex Systems & Network Science

This stream studies the robustness, vulnerability and resilience of air transportation networks, including delay propagation, congestion, critical links and bottlenecks using graph theory, percolation methods and large-scale simulation.

Sustainability & Green Aviation

Research includes continuous descent operations, top-of-descent optimisation, surface efficiency and emissions-aware traffic management. Earlier policy work included leading the National Aviation Emissions Action Plan for the Kingdom of Bahrain submitted to ICAO.

Operational impact

From models to regulation and operations

MIDRAS, an airspace collision-risk modelling system arising from my research, has been adopted by the ICAO Middle East Regional Monitoring Agency for safety assessment across the region.

My research has also informed discussions and programmes involving CAAS, ICAO, EASA, EUROCONTROL, FAA, ASEAN and SESAR.
