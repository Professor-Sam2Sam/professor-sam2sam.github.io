Research

AI, complex systems and air transportation

Research focused on the design, analysis and deployment of intelligent methods for complex, safety-critical transportation systems.

01

Artificial Intelligence for Air Traffic Management

AI-based methods for traffic prediction, optimisation and control under uncertainty, including trajectory prediction, air traffic flow management, airport and airside operations, urban air mobility and mixed-mode airspace integration.

02

Human–AI Hybrid Systems

Human–AI collaboration in safety-critical operations, including trust, workload, situational awareness, explainability and human-in-the-loop validation.

03

Complex Systems & Network Science

Network robustness, vulnerability, congestion propagation, critical links and resilience in large-scale air transportation systems.

04

Sustainability & Green Aviation

Continuous descent operations, top-of-descent optimisation, taxi-time reduction, surface efficiency and emissions-aware traffic management.

Impact

From scientific models to operational use

My work has informed national and international aviation stakeholders including CAAS, ICAO, EASA, EUROCONTROL, FAA, ASEAN and SESAR.

MIDRAS, an airspace collision-risk modelling system arising from my research, has been used by the ICAO Middle East Regional Monitoring Agency for regional safety assessment.
