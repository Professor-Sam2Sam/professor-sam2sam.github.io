Selected themes

Research across science, engineering and policy

01

AI for Air Traffic Management

Machine learning, reinforcement learning, graph models and probabilistic methods for prediction, optimisation and control.

02

Human–AI Hybrid Systems

Trust, workload, explainability, cognitive engineering and human-in-the-loop validation in safety-critical environments.

03

Complex Systems & Networks

Resilience, congestion propagation, vulnerability and capacity in large-scale air transportation networks.

04

Sustainable Aviation

Continuous descent, surface efficiency, emissions-aware operations and data-driven decision support.

Leadership

Building research teams, partnerships and programmes

As Director of ATMRI, I lead multidisciplinary research spanning faculty, research staff, students and professional teams, with extensive engagement across government, regulators, industry and international research organisations.

Current and recent programmes include the S$13.457M Future Airspace Architecture programme, European SESAR research programmes, ICAO and ASEAN regional studies, and the Saab–NTU Joint Research Laboratory.

Academic philosophy
Strong applied outcomes are built on strong foundational science — and the most valuable research is research that can withstand both scientific scrutiny and real-world use.
Education

Connecting theory with practice

My teaching includes Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Air Traffic Management, Airport Operations and Air Transportation. I use simulation, mixed reality, digital twins and industry-linked projects to connect scientific principles with operational challenges.
