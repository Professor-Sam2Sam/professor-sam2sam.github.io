Sameer Alam

Associate Professor and Director of the Air Traffic Management Research Institute (ATMRI) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, air traffic management, complex systems, human–AI collaboration and sustainable aviation. Across more than two decades, I have worked to connect fundamental science with operational aviation, public policy and international regulation.

Research

AI for complex, safety-critical transportation systems

AI for Air Traffic Management

Machine learning, reinforcement learning, graph models and probabilistic methods for prediction, optimisation and control.

Human–AI Hybrid Systems

Trust, workload, situational awareness, explainability and human-in-the-loop validation for safety-critical operations.

Complex Systems & Networks

Airspace resilience, network vulnerability, congestion propagation and critical-link analysis.

Sustainable Aviation

Continuous descent operations, surface efficiency, emissions-aware traffic management and system-level optimisation.

Leadership & impact

Research that moves beyond publication

I lead multidisciplinary programmes connecting universities, regulators and industry. The portfolio includes the S$13.45M Future Airspace Architecture programme and participation in European SESAR research programmes HyperSolver and DeepFlow.

My work has also contributed to regulatory and operational practice, including ICAO collision-risk assessment in the Middle East, EASA work on AI assurance, and national aviation sustainability policy.

Education

Future-ready aviation and AI education

My teaching connects scientific foundations with operational problems using simulation, industry-linked projects, mixed reality and digital twins.

I introduced MAE's first core course in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence and developed specialised courses in Air Traffic Management, Airport Operations and Air Transportation.
